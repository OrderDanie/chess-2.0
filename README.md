# chess-2.0
as if chess 1 wasnt enough

What if chess,was a tbs tactical war game? Yeaaaa that’s chess 2

Chess 2 is a turn-based strategy game that combines chess with 4X systems

You capture territory, build farms, grow your economy, spawn units, and fight for control of the map. It is not just about moving pieces well. You also need to expand, defend key areas, manage resources, and deal with diplomacy

The game will have both singleplayer and multiplayer, with matches built around map control, army management, and long-term decision making

Build your kingdom from the ground up by capturing land, gathering resources, and constructing buildings. Use your economy to recruit new units and strengthen your army

Every tile matters. Control important areas, protect your resources, and plan your attacks carefully. Expand too fast and you might leave your kingdom defenseless

Play against AI opponents or challenge other players in multiplayer. Adapt your strategy, outsmart your enemies, and find your own path to victory

The goal is simple. Build your empire, dominate the battlefield, and conquer the map. Because apparently normal chess wasnt complicated enough
